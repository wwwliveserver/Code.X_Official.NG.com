Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Here are some answers to the most common queries about my services. From timelines and pricing to website features and maintenance, it’s all covered. If you don’t see your question here, feel free to reach out directly.

1. How long does it usually take to complete a project?

The timeline depends on the complexity of the website. Simple websites can typically be completed within 1–4 weeks, while more complex or custom-built websites may take longer, depending on the number of pages, features, and revisions required.

2. Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes! Every website comes with 1 month of free maintenance to handle updates, bug fixes, and ensure everything runs smoothly. After that, ongoing maintenance can be continued for an additional fee, tailored to your needs.

3. Do you help with domain registration or hosting?

Absolutely. I can guide you through choosing and registering a domain name, set up hosting, and even configure your website so it’s live and secure. I make the process simple, so you don’t have to worry about the technical details.

4. Do you require a deposit before starting a project?

Yes, a deposit is required to get started, with the balance due upon completion.

5. Will the website be mobile-friendly and responsive?

Yes! All websites are usually built to be fully responsive, but specific features or layouts can be adjusted depending on your preferences.
